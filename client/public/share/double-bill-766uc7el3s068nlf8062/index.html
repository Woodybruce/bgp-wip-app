Double Bill, the brand

A double bill is two films for one ticket. A Double Bill park is the film and the rest of the night on one site. Park names stay; Double Bill signs the landlord line. Cleared at Companies House on 21 Oct 2026 (Encore and Foyer are taken); trade-mark search still to run.

Marks

Two ticket stubs, fanned: ink behind, house-lights amber in front. Minimum height 24px for the mark, 32px for the wordmark. Clear space of one stub all round. The amber is never recoloured.

Voice

  • Short sentences. Plain words. British spelling.
  • Talk about nights out, not footfall; occupiers by name, never 'tenants' on anything a visitor sees.
  • One number per sentence. The mark does the shouting; the copy stays calm.
  • Never 'destination', 'experiential' or 'curated'.
  • Parks keep their own names (Valley Centertainment, BH2, Coliseum). Double Bill signs the landlord line: 'A Double Bill park'.
