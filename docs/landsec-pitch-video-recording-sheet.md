# Landsec pitch video: recording sheet for Woody

Three recordings. For everything except the walking clip: a quiet room, and
the phone on a stand or propped up, not hand held.

## Recording 1: you on camera (two clips)

**Clip A, the opening, to camera.** Phone sideways (landscape), lens at eye
level, a window in front of you, a plain wall behind. Look at the lens, not
the screen. Two or three takes; keep them all.

> I'm Woody Bruce from Bruce Gillingham Pollard. You asked, why our agency.
> Part of the answer is something we've built for you, so let me show you
> rather than tell you. In fact, I'll get someone else to show you.

**Clip B, the ending, walking with the phone.** Someone films you (sideways
again) walking towards them with the app open in your hand. Scroll it for a
few seconds as you walk, then look up and say the close. Somewhere quiet
enough to hear you: a corridor or a street without traffic. Two or three
takes.

> That is one unit at one scheme. It runs the same way across all fourteen,
> so our time goes on brands and deals, not on chasing updates. We'd love to
> run it with you.

## Recording 2: the voice (one voice memo)

Same room, phone about a hand's width from your mouth. Read each line as its
own take: say the number, pause, read the line, pause two seconds, move on.
If you fluff one, just say the number again and re-read it. An easy,
unhurried pace.

**2.** That's better. Mark, this is ChatBGP, and this is your login. One place
for all fourteen schemes.

**3.** Every unit, every vacancy and every live letting, counted the same way
for your portfolio team, your asset teams, us and the other agents on the
panel. Let's take Bluewater.

**4.** The plan and the tenancy schedule are one thing. Click a unit and
everything about it is there. This is Burger King on the lower level: three
thousand square feet, with the lease ending in December 2028. Line up what
comes next now, and there is no downtime.

**5.** Differentiation starts with knowing what the competition has. The app
tracks who trades at Lakeside, Stratford and Canary Wharf and not at
Bluewater, and what those centres are doing each week.

**6.** And it tracks who is growing. Jersey Mike's: new to the UK, backed by
Blackstone, with a stated plan for four hundred sites. A first to market for
Bluewater, found on evidence.

**7.** Before anyone picks up the phone, the brief is agreed. Rent,
incentives, target brands. Mark for leasing, Rob as asset manager, and
Charlotte, Millie and Ben for us, each in their own login.

**8.** Then it runs on the Letting Tracker. An offer comes in and we type it
the way we'd say it. The app fills in the form and measures it against the
brief.

**9.** Heads of Terms come out on Bluewater's own form, and everyone signs.

**10.** From Heads of Terms the asset team takes over, with a name against
every step. At any moment you can see what the deal is waiting on, and who.

**11.** The recommendation arrives in your own template, with Net Effective
Rent worked out against ERV and the brief.

**12.** Viewings, meetings and tasks sit in one place.

**13.** So does the conversation on each property, for you and for every
agent on the instruction.

**14.** You also asked how we work with the other agents on the panel. Here
comes one now. This is David Bell. We call him Tinker Bell. And yes, we do
like him.

**15.** David gets his own login, with his schemes and nothing else. The same
tracker, the same checklist, the same conversation. So you see one version of
every deal, whoever did the work.

**16.** And it all goes with you. Your schemes, your deals, and what each one
is waiting on, on your phone.

## Recording 3: footage for the character

The avatar service will ask for a short piece of you talking to camera to
build the character from, usually a minute or two, plus a consent clip it
words for you. Check what the one you choose asks for before you film, and do
it in the same sitting as Recording 1.

## Sending them back

Attach the files to the chat. If the videos are too big, put them in the
Landsec Pitch 2026 folder on SharePoint and say so.
