1.0 Introduction
Troup Bywaters + Anders (TB+A) have been appointed by Lighthouse Surveyors and
commissioned to undertake a review of the engineering services installations at the Bank of
England Sports Centre, London SW15 5JQ.
The Bank of England Sports Centre covers approx. 32 acres and includes Commercial office
space, administration and members facilities including commercial kitchens and restaurant, bars
and sports facilities including residential cottages and grounds staff outbuildings
This report has been prepared to provide a general appraisal of the engineering services installed
within the sports centre complex in consideration of a potential purchase of the leasehold to the
property. This report is solely for the property named as the Sports Hall and Swimming Pool.
Squash Courts, Bank Lane & Clarence Farm Cottages.

Troup Bywaters + Anders (TB+A) have been appointed by Lighthouse Surveyors and

The Bank of England Sports Centre covers approx. 32 acres and includes Commercial office

This report has been prepared to provide a general appraisal of the engineering services installed
within the sports centre complex in consideration of a potential purchase of the leasehold to the
Bank Lane & Clarence Farm Cottages and Sports Hall including Swimming Pool.
1.1TheProperty (Pavilionand Marquee)
Located off of Priory Lane, Roehampton, South West London is a two-storey building known as the
restaurant, hot service servery, bars, meeting rooms, offices and sports changing facilities with a
GlA of 25,800sq.ft. A permanent marquee is located to the north of the Pavilion and is a single
storey open plan structure with a GlA of 3,900sq.ft. The Pavilions ventilation and air conditioning
plant equipment located to level 1 external flat roof areas and the main LTHW Boiler Plant Room is
air conditioning and is located to the rear of the marquee. Non-potable and potable water storage
tanks are located within the pavilion central roof space loft.

1.2 Limitations
No inspection was undertaken to those parts of the building services which are built in, covered up
or otherwise inaccessible. The survey consisted of a non-invasive visual survey - no building
services equipment covers were removed or ceiling tiles/plant sealed risers disturbed.
No definitive tests or calculations have been undertaken to determine the capacity of the plant nor
have performance tests been carried out on the plant and system.
No functional testing of systems or installations was performed.
No access was gained to any underground services.
No access was possible to Pavilion kitchen high level flat roof, Pavilion North and South Wing loft
space which were inaccessible. (Note: The Pavilion north and south wing roof plant is only
accessible by loft space crawling boards - this is a health and safety issue as a working at heights
issue).
